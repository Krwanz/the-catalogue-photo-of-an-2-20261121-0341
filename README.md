A catalogue photograph of an impossible gadget: PORTA-DOOR, a folding door
to anywhere. A single modern white folding door stands in a clean studio;
through its open doorway is a tropical beach in Bali, while the studio
around it stays plain white. A folded copy leans in its box beside it, and
the make.fun ghost — tourist hat, sunglasses, suitcase — is about to walk
through.

Entry for the make.fun bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/the-catalogue-photo-of-an-2-20261121-0341

## v2

The door stands open wider than in v1, so the Bali beach — turquoise sea,
palms and sand — fills the whole doorway and the impossible destination
reads at a single glance. The keypad display is larger now, so
"DESTINATION: BALI - 0 SECONDS" is clearly legible.

## v3

Golden hour in Bali: warm sunset light fills the doorway in strong
contrast with the cool white studio, so the beach is the first thing the
eye hits. The impossible destination now leaks out of the door — a small
drift of white sand spills onto the studio floor and a single palm frond
leans through the frame — proof the door really opens onto Bali, while
the studio stays a clean catalogue background.

## How it works

`catalogue.png` is the finished 819×1024 catalogue photo (under 1 MiB).
`main.ts` copies it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Run it locally:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
